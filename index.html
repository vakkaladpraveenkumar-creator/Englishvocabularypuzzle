Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the English Vocabulary Puzzle mobile application (the "App") developed by Praveen Kumar Vakkalad.

Information Collection and Use

The App is a free educational English vocabulary puzzle game. It does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

The App works fully offline for its core features. No personal information is stored or transmitted by the App itself.

Advertising

The App may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the following data to show and measure ads:

  • Advertising ID (AAID / Advertising Identifier)
  • IP address (used to estimate approximate location)
  • Device information
  • App interactions and ad engagement data

This data is used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. You can limit or reset ad tracking in your device settings.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Children’s Privacy

The App is suitable for children and educational use. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob. We do not control the data practices of third-party services. Please review their privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
